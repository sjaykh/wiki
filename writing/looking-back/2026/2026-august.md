When the voice starts

It will call something a waste of time. It will predict you will never get better. It will suggest putting it into work instead, where you already have proof.

Notice that it never recommends work because work is better. It recommends the place with evidence, because the other place has none. That is fear wearing a sensible voice.

John Nash never made the hallucinations go away. He stopped doing what they said.

Your own question

What is the worst that can happen?

You wrote that this single question has shaped more of your path than you realised. Use it. Most of the time the honest answer is: something is mediocre and one person knows.

Two things to stop doing

Waiting for certainty before starting. It is not on offer, to anyone, ever. The certainty is the price the fear is charging, set deliberately at a level that cannot be paid.

Grading the recovery. If you catch yourself assessing how well you are handling this, that is the same rule in new clothes. The goal is not to become better. It is to become less dependent on being better.
